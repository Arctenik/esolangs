Alkmini
A compiler from Alkmini to Kwert.

Tip: To identify Alkmini symbols in the Kwert program, wait until the program halts or until the number of Kwert cycles evaluated is any multiple of 5, and then look for sequences consisting of a symbol command (s_<symbol name>, with some number of underscores) followed by a padding pre-generator command (padpg, potentially with trailing underscores).
